# USER MANUAL - THM LabTrack

## 1. Purpose
THM LabTrack organizes TryHackMe lab assignments, student submissions, and manager reviews in one web application.

## 2. Student Instructions
1. Sign in with your Student account.
2. View assigned labs on your dashboard.
3. Complete the lab on TryHackMe.
4. Submit the URL and upload the screenshot proof.
5. **Self-Practice:** Use the "+ Submit Self-Practice Lab" button to submit labs not assigned by the manager.
6. Check your points and leaderboard ranking on the dashboard.

## 3. Manager (Admin) Instructions
1. Sign in with the Manager account.
2. Create new assignments, set deadlines, and assign them to specific batches.
3. Review student submissions in the "Submission Review" table.
4. Verify or Reject submissions (Feedback required for rejection).
5. Export data using the "Export CSV report" button.

## 4. Understanding Statuses
* **Pending:** Submission recorded, waiting for review.
* **Verified:** Manager accepted the work. Student gets 10 points.
* **Rejected:** Work not accepted. Check feedback and resubmit.

## 5. Troubleshooting
* **Image upload fails:** Check internet connection and Cloudinary settings.
* **Page looks outdated:** Refresh or re-login.
* **Access denied:** Confirm your account role.
